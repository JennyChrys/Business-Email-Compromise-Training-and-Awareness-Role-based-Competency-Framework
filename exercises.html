How the score works

Red-flag rounds: red flags found out of the red flags in the email. Decision games: attempt stopped scores 1, partly right 0.5, fraud succeeded 0. Knowledge checks: correct scores 1. The practice score is the average of the activities you have done. It is practice feedback, not a test.
