Impact

How the training helps you in your work

The aim is a change in the decision you make at the moment of the request: a request that looks routine becomes a prompt to pause, verify somewhere the request cannot reach, and report.

What changes

From complying to checking

Without role-specific practice
A request arrives›It looks routine›You comply›Money or access is lost
With the training
A request arrives›You recognise the levers›You verify it elsewhere›Reported and contained
How it helps

Four things you take back to your desk

One habit, practised in your role

Every module teaches the same rule: verify somewhere the request can't reach. The example and the control change with your role.

Decisions, not slides

Two decision games in every module, one by phone and one on video. Each choice has a consequence and a debrief.

Psychology made visible

After every red-flag round, the levers that were pulled are named, so you can notice authority, urgency and the rest as they happen.

Speed when it counts

A single contain flow asks whether money or data has already moved. Calling the bank first matters: in 2025 the FBI froze 58% of the attempted theft in its recovery cases, across all fraud types.
