The research basis

The ten BEC patterns this training is built on

The modules are built on a taxonomy of ten BEC attack patterns. Each pattern is matched to the psychological triggers it exploits and the roles it most often targets. Choose a role to see which patterns reach it.

The model

Every module follows the same three phases

A request travels through Detect, Verify and Contain. The structure is shared so the habit transfers between roles; the examples, the levers and the controls are specific to each one.
