Six roles

Why six roles, not one course

Attackers do not target an organisation evenly. They target the person whose decision produces the outcome they want.

Why six roles

Training should rehearse the decision

A payment released, a bank detail changed, an account unlocked, a record disclosed. Each role makes a different high-risk decision, so each gets its own module.

Different decisions

Each role can complete a different high-risk action. Accounts Payable releases payments, Payroll changes pay details, and IT grants access.

Different lures

The same psychological levers appear in every attack, but the story changes with the role: an overdue invoice, an urgent direct-deposit change, a locked-out colleague.

Different controls

The check that works also differs: a callback to the vendor master file, a second approver, a break-glass procedure. Each module teaches the control that role actually uses.
